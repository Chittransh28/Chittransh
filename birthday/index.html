08 · From me, to you

Khushi,

Sometimes I genuinely think about how lucky I am that you came into my life, and I don't know if I'll ever find words big enough for what you mean to me.

I've known you for so many years now, and somehow I still find new things to love about you. I love your beauty, obviously. Your smile, your eyes, your hair, your style — all of it. But what makes you so much more than just beautiful is the person behind all of that.

I love how caring you are. I love how you look after me, how protective you can be, and how deeply you love the people who matter to you. I love your sense of humour, your little moods, your softness, your fire, and even that overthinking brain of yours that sometimes makes both of us want to pull our hair out. 🫠

I hope you know how valuable you are. Not because of how you look, not because of what you achieve, and not because of what anyone else thinks of you. You are valuable because of the person you are. You are impressive because you keep going. You are capable because you've already handled things that once seemed impossible. And you are strong, even on the days when you don't feel strong at all.

I know a new place, new people and all the uncertainty that comes with them can make you question yourself sometimes. I know you overthink. I know there are moments when you wonder whether you're enough or whether you're seeing people correctly. I just hope that, somewhere underneath all those thoughts, you remember the girl I see.

I see someone incredibly beautiful, deeply loved, wonderfully imperfect, ridiculously cute, strong, capable, caring and full of potential. I see someone who deserves good things, good people, beautiful experiences and a life that makes her genuinely happy.

And I feel incredibly fortunate that I get to know that girl. Fortunate that I get to love her. Fortunate that after all these years, I still get to call her mine.

I can't promise that every year will be easy. But I can promise that I'll always be cheering for you, believing in you, annoying you, making fun of you, worrying about you, and reminding you of your worth whenever you forget it.

So today, don't worry about anything else for a while. Dress up. Eat your favourite food. Take a ridiculous number of pictures. Laugh a lot. Enjoy being celebrated.

Because you deserve it.

Happy Birthday, my love.
I hope you see yourself someday the way I see you. 💜

Always yours,
Me.
